## Matching limitations

- Apple points to the same avocado image in the workbook; no apple photo assigned.
- The fresh mojito image anchors overlap; strawberry, passion and blackberry were identified visually. No mixed fruit image was supplied.
- Workbook nachos are labelled vegetarian while the Nairobi dish is chicken; no photo assigned.
- Salmon salad, oats, spicy wedges, nano mixed platter, cappuccino and tenderloin steak photos have no exact corresponding entry in the Nairobi menu.
- Nutella Cream Latte is not assigned to Nutella Caramel Latte because the flavours differ.
- Items absent from the workbook retain no photo.

## Display policy

Items without an image are temporarily hidden using the filtered exports in menu.ts; all 243 entries remain in menu-final.json. Empty categories are hidden. Assign a photo path to restore the item automatically. Five pizza items use linked online example photos with creator and license links displayed on their cards. The peri-peri example is a generic spicy chicken pizza, and the steak example does not establish the cut of beef. Replace these example URLs with TeaTime photos when available.
